Privacy Policy

The current version requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking. It does not transmit personal information, device identifiers, or gameplay records to a server.

Progress such as the personal best is stored only on the device and may be removed when the app is uninstalled. If these practices change, this page and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated.
