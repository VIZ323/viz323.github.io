# App Store Copy (English US)

## Name

Frog Hop Home

## Subtitle

Hold, release, and land the leap

## Keywords

frog,jump,lily pad,casual,endless,offline,cozy,reflex,one touch

## Promotional Text

A one-finger pond adventure. Charge each jump, land with precision, and beat your personal best.

## Description

The next lily pad is waiting.

Hold the screen to charge, then release to jump automatically. The controls take one finger, but every landing asks for good timing and a steady touch. The farther you travel, the more the pond tests your rhythm.

Features:

- Simple hold-and-release controls
- An endless pond with gradual difficulty
- Precision landing streaks and firefly rewards
- Sinking lily pads and rotating run goals
- A locally saved personal best
- Fully offline play with no account required

Take it one lily pad at a time and see how far you can go.

## Review Notes

No account or network connection is required. From the home screen, tap the start button, hold the play area to charge, and release to jump. All core gameplay is immediately available with no hidden login or paywall.
